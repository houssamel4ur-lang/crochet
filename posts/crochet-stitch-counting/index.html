5 Easy Ways to Keep Your Crochet Stitches Even

2026-11-03 · CozyLoops

Simple habits to help avoid uneven edges, lost stitches and frustrating do-overs.

On this page
  1. Count Every Row
  2. Use Stitch Markers
  3. Watch Your Tension
  4. Check Turning Chains
  5. Practice Slowly

Count Every Row

Count stitches at the end of each row, especially when learning a new pattern.

Use Stitch Markers

Mark the first and last stitch to help keep edges straight.

Watch Your Tension

Hold yarn comfortably and try to keep your loops consistent without pulling too tightly.

Check Turning Chains

Read whether the pattern counts the turning chain as a stitch.

Practice Slowly

Accuracy comes before speed. Small swatches are an excellent way to build muscle memory.
