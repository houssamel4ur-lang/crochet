Mini Cat Amigurumi: Pocket Size

By Emma Carter · Published December 9, 2026 · Updated December 9, 2026 · 6 min read

Crochet a pocket-size cat in one evening — a sitting amigurumi about 8 cm tall, with pointy ears, a curled tail and an optional keychain loop.

A pocket cat is the smallest useful amigurumi you can make in one evening: a sitting cat about 8 cm tall, worked in single crochet from a magic ring, with pointy ears, a curled tail and a keychain loop if you want one. It is a single standalone cat, not one of a set, and the only shaping is the same increase and decrease you already know.

Quick Project Overview

What you will learn: A magic ring, single crochet increases and decreases, joining small pieces, and an embroidered face. Skill level: Easy. Terminology: US. Finished size: about 8 cm tall sitting, ears included, and about 3.5 cm wide. Time: about 2 hours.

Materials and Tools

DK yarn (#3): about 20 g in the main colour, plus a small amount in a contrast colour for the inner ears; a 3 mm crochet hook; two safety eyes, 6 mm across; a small amount of toy stuffing; a yarn needle; stitch markers; scissors; pink embroidery thread for the nose; one split ring, about 20 mm across (optional, for the keychain loop).

Stitches and Gauge

Stitches used: chain (ch), slip stitch (sl st), single crochet (sc). Increase (inc): 2 sc in one stitch. Decrease (dec): sc2tog. Gauge: 22 sc and 24 rows measure 10 cm with a 3 mm hook. Mark the first stitch of every round — one missed increase makes the whole cat lean.

The Pattern

Body: Round 1: Magic ring, 6 sc. (6)

Round 2: Inc in each stitch around. (12)

Round 3: *Sc, inc; repeat from * around. (18)

Round 4: *2 sc, inc; repeat from * around. (24)

Rounds 5–7: Sc in each stitch around. (24)

Round 8: *2 sc, dec; repeat from * around. (18)

Round 9: *Sc, dec; repeat from * around. (12) Stuff lightly now — firm enough to sit, not so full the stitches stretch open.

Round 10: Dec around. (6) Fasten off and close. The body is about 4 cm tall and 3.5 cm wide.

Head: Round 1: Magic ring, 6 sc. (6)

Round 2: Inc in each stitch around. (12)

Round 3: *Sc, inc; repeat from * around. (18)

Round 4: *2 sc, inc; repeat from * around. (24)

Rounds 5–6: Sc in each stitch around. (24)

Round 7: *2 sc, dec; repeat from * around. (18) Place the safety eyes between Rounds 5 and 6, 6 stitches apart.

Round 8: *Sc, dec; repeat from * around. (12) Stuff lightly.

Round 9: Dec around. (6) Fasten off and close. Sew the head to the top of the body, centred, eyes facing forward.

Ears (make 2): Work Rounds 1–2 in the contrast colour for pink inner ears, then switch to the main colour.

Round 1: Magic ring, 4 sc. (4)

Round 2: *Sc, inc; repeat from * once more. (6)

Round 3: *2 sc, inc; repeat from * once more. (8)

Round 4: Sc in each stitch around. (8) Fasten off, flatten the ear, and sew it to the top of the head, pink side forward.

Tail: Magic ring, 6 sc, then 4 rounds of sc around (6). The tube is about 2 cm long. Fasten off and sew it to the back of the body, curving it up toward the head.

Legs (make 2): Magic ring, 6 sc, then 2 rounds of sc around (6). Fasten off and sew them to the front of the body, side by side, so the cat sits upright on its flat base.

Face: With pink thread, stitch a small triangle nose between the eyes, two short lines for the mouth, and three short whiskers on each side.

Keychain loop (optional): Chain 12, slip stitch into the top of the head between the ears to form a loop about 5 cm around. Thread the split ring through it — the loop lets the ring move freely instead of sawing through one spot.

Styling and Care

The cat sits on a desk, a shelf, or a keychain. Hand wash cold and dry flat, reshaping the ears while it is damp. Do not tumble dry: heat makes the stuffing clump and the cat goes lopsided. Safety eyes are not for children under 3 — for a baby gift, embroider the eyes instead.

Frequently Asked Questions

How small can I make it? Use 4-ply yarn and a 2.5 mm hook with the same round counts, and the cat comes out about 6–7 cm tall. Go smaller than that and the safety eyes no longer fit — use 5 mm eyes, or embroider them. Do not just work tighter with the same yarn: tight stitches with a 3 mm hook make a stiff cat that will not sit flat.

Can I put it on a keychain? Yes — the pattern includes a chain-12 loop at the top of the head and a 20 mm split ring. Sew the loop through both layers of the head so it cannot pull out, and use a proper split ring, not a jump ring that can bend open.

What yarn keeps the shape? Cotton or acrylic DK. Cotton holds its shape best and the stitches stay crisp; acrylic is softer and a little springier. Avoid bamboo blends and chenille: they are too soft, the ears flop, and the cat will not sit up. Whatever you use, stuff firmly — a soft cat slumps, a firm cat sits.

Can I make it in other colours? Yes — the same rounds make any cat. For a tabby, change colour every 2 rounds on the body and head; for a calico, work the head in one colour and the body in another; for a black cat, use a light colour for the nose and whiskers so the face shows.
