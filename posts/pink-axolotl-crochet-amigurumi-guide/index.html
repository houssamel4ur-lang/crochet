Pink Axolotl Crochet: Beginner Amigurumi Body and Frilled Gills

By CozyLoops

Make a cute pink crochet axolotl with a rounded body, little feet, frilled gills and embroidered face; follow clear stitch counts.

An axolotl has a recognizable friendly shape: a round head and body with six feathery external gills. The gills carry the character, so this tutorial emphasizes secure assembly and consistent placement. The decorative pattern is not independently toy-safety tested.

Quick Project Overview

What you will learn: Make a cute pink crochet axolotl with a rounded body, little feet, frilled gills and embroidered face; follow clear stitch counts. Skill level: Beginner to confident beginner depending on shaping and assembly. Terminology: US. Size: Make a gauge swatch; finished size depends on yarn, hook and tension.

Materials and Tools

DK cotton in blush and deeper pink; 2.75–3 mm hook; soft filling; tapestry needle; black embroidery thread; markers.

Crochet Abbreviations and a Practice Swatch

  • ch: chain; sc: single crochet; hdc: half double crochet; dc: double crochet; tr: treble crochet.
  • sl st: slip stitch; inc: two stitches worked into one; dec: decrease; MR: magic ring; BLO: back loop only.
  • [number]: number of stitches expected after completing a row or round. A turning chain counts only when the instructions explicitly say so.

For standardized terms see the Craft Yarn Council crochet abbreviations. Before shaping a project, make a small swatch and count ten consecutive stitches. Compare your tension with any measurements stated below instead of assuming every crocheter produces the same size.

Free Pattern or Construction Tutorial: Step by Step

Main body: R1 6 sc in MR (6); R2 inc x6 (12); R3 (sc, inc) x6 (18); R4 (2 sc, inc) x6 (24); R5 (3 sc, inc) x6 (30); R6–R11 sc around (30); R12 (3 sc, dec) x6 (24); R13 (2 sc, dec) x6 (18); R14 (sc, dec) x6 (12). Embroider facial features before the opening narrows; add light stuffing. R15 dec x6 (6); draw closed. Six gill fronds: In dark pink, ch 8, sc in second chain, then work sc, hdc, dc, hdc, sc, sl st across remaining chains. Fasten off. Make six. Four stubby legs: Ch 5, sc back along four chains, leaving long sewing tails. Make four. Assembly: Sew three gills on each side of the upper body, positioned away from the face; sew legs below the body with several passes each.

How to Shape and Assemble It Neatly

Lay the finished pieces on a flat work surface before you sew. Match the front and back, identify a center point, and secure the most visible details only after you are satisfied with alignment. Sew through more than one small stitch when attaching parts that will be handled or moved.

Embroider eyes instead of using plastic safety eyes for small parts. Even embroidered details do not automatically make a handmade toy safe for babies.

Finishing and Durability Checklist

A little stuffing gives rounded shape without widening gaps between stitches. Use a hook small enough to hide the fill.

Before using or gifting the project, weave tails deeply into matching-color stitches, trim only after confirming the finish is secure, and inspect for uneven tension, protruding knots or fraying. Wash or block only as permitted by the yarn label. Do not claim a design is child-safe or load-bearing without the relevant testing.

Sizing and Customization

Keep the side gills evenly spaced. Test pin placement first and sew the two highest on opposite sides before the rest, then embroider the smile centrally.

Troubleshooting: Avoid These Mistakes

If the head appears too tall, count the six straight rounds before decreasing. If the gills flop, shorten the foundation chains or sew across a wider base.

  • Unexpected stitch count: Check whether a turning chain was counted as a stitch. Mark the first stitch in rounds.
  • Loose finishing: Pass the yarn needle through several fabric sections before clipping the tail.
  • Project looks different from the photograph: Yarn texture, hook size, tension and finishing all affect the result; AI visuals are styling references, not proof of exact construction.

Styling, Gift Ideas and Next Projects

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I turn it into a keychain?

Only after reducing its size and securely attaching a durable ring; long gills can snag.

What does inc mean?

Two single crochets into one stitch.

Is this beginner-friendly?

Yes if you can crochet in the round and sew on small parts.

Can I sell the finished project?

You may use the instructions as a starting point for making your own work, but verify measurements, construction, durability and applicable product safety requirements before selling. Photograph the exact product you made, especially if you advertise the design or its size.

Final Notes

If the project will be handled by children, conduct appropriate safety assessment rather than assuming a handmade item is automatically safe.
