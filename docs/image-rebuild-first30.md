## Quality gates

- Each shot must feature the EXACT article subject, color/material profile and intended project anatomy; no unrelated crochet props impersonating tutorial steps.
- One image per file. No montage, no lettering, no badges, no brand logo, no icons, no watermarks, no fictional progress indicators.
- Make scenes feel like clean, realistic craft product photographs with clear yarn texture and enough margin around the subject; do not clip leaves, ears, straps, flower petals or project edges.
- Minimum 1200 × 750 pixels, genuine WebP files (prefer 1600 × 1000 or greater). Do not upscale small contact-sheet crops to pass this test.
- Use four independent scenes per article: `hero.webp` (finished object), `materials.webp` (specified yarn/tools), `construction.webp` (project-accurate intermediate stage), `finishing.webp` (seams/attachments/final styling).
- Preserve a source image and do not automatically remove a user-uploaded custom photo.
- AI concepts must remain disclosed as AI concepts; no claim of actual tested stitch-by-stitch photographs.
- A manual visual review is required even when the automated size/URL tests pass.

### 1. Crochet Daisy Bookmark: Free Flower Pattern and Stem Tutorial

- **Slug:** `crochet-daisy-flower-bookmark-free-pattern`
- **Materials to show:** Sport-weight cotton in yellow, white and leaf green; a 2.5–3 mm hook; yarn needle; scissors; removable marker; favorite paperback for fit.
- **Construction details to respect:** **Center:** With yellow, make 6 sc in a magic ring (6). R2: 2 sc in every stitch (12); sl st to the first stitch and fasten off. **Petals:** Join white in any center stitch. Repeat six times: ch 4, work 1 sc in the second chain, 1 hdc in the next chain and 1 dc in the last chain; sl st into the center stitch immediately after the next one, working evenly around the circle. This creates six narrow petals, each anchored across two center stitches. Check that you use 12 center stitches in total. **Stem:** With green ch 36 (or desired length); work sl st back along each chain for a firm cord. Sew the cord to the flower back with several passes. **Leaf:** ch 8, sc in second chain, hdc, dc, dc, hdc, sc, sl st. Fasten off and sew at one side of the stem.
- **Deliverables:** `assets/article-photos-v2/crochet-daisy-flower-bookmark-free-pattern/{hero,materials,construction,finishing}.webp`

### 2. Crochet Phone Pouch: Adjustable Crossbody Pattern and Size Guide

- **Slug:** `crochet-phone-pouch-crossbody-size-guide`
- **Materials to show:** Worsted cotton or cotton blend; 3.5–4 mm hook; stitch markers; tapestry needle; tape measure; optional lining fabric and sewing thread.
- **Construction details to respect:** **Gauge first:** Work a swatch of single crochet; record stitches per centimeter. **Front and back:** Chain the number needed for your phone width plus 1–2 cm ease. Make a foundation row of sc in the second chain and across. Ch 1, turn (chain does not count); sc in every stitch for each row. Work until each rectangle covers your phone height plus 2 cm. Make two identical panels and compare measurements. **Join:** Put wrong sides together and seam the two side edges and bottom with slip stitches or mattress stitch, keeping the upper edge open. **Top edging:** Sc around the opening, spacing stitches evenly; check the phone still enters without stretching. **Strap:** Ch your preferred crossbody length, slip stitch back along the chain (or crochet 2–3 rows of sc to make a wider strap), and reinforce both ends through at least 4 body stitches.
- **Deliverables:** `assets/article-photos-v2/crochet-phone-pouch-crossbody-size-guide/{hero,materials,construction,finishing}.webp`

### 3. Crochet Flower Coasters: Easy Cotton Pattern with Flat Petals

### 4. Crochet Tulip Bouquet: Petal Pattern, Leaves and Gift Wrapping

- **Slug:** `crochet-tulip-bouquet-stems-guide`
- **Materials to show:** Sport cotton in flower and leaf colors; 2.5–3 mm hook; needle; stuffing scraps; paper-covered floral wire or flexible craft stems; florists’ tape; kraft paper.

Work back on the other side of the foundation chain in mirrored order to form an oval leaf. ### 5. Crochet Butterfly Ornament: Two Wings, Body and Hanging Loop

- **Slug:** `crochet-butterfly-ornament-wing-guide`
- **Materials to show:** DK cotton in two wing colors; 3 mm hook; tapestry needle; stitch markers; embroidery thread; optional hanging thread.
- **Construction details to respect:** **Make two wings:** Ch 4, join with sl st into a ring. R1: ch 3 (counts as first dc), 11 dc into ring, join (12 dc total). R2: [ch 2, 2 dc in next stitch, 2 dc in next stitch, ch 2, sl st in next stitch] x4. This creates four rounded lobes; use the same tension on both wing pieces. **Body:** Ch 12; sc in second chain and each remaining chain (11 sc), fasten off. Fold or lightly roll the strip lengthwise and stitch together to make a raised body. **Assembly:** Place the two wing motifs with their inner sides overlapping slightly behind the body. Sew through the central ring of both motifs and several body stitches. **Antennae:** Tie two short strands at the top, knot ends and trim neatly. **Hanging loop:** Add a separate thread anchored through the top body stitches.
- **Deliverables:** `assets/article-photos-v2/crochet-butterfly-ornament-wing-guide/{hero,materials,construction,finishing}.webp`
