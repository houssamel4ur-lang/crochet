Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 16, 2026

Website operator and data controller: Houssal El Ouardy, operator of CozyLoops (cozyloops.site). Contact: Houssam.el.8ur@gmail.com.

What information we collect

CozyLoops can be browsed without creating an account. We do not currently process purchases or newsletter sign-ups through this site. When you contact us by email or through the contact form, your message, address and any information you provide may be processed to answer your enquiry. Form submissions are sent to FormSubmit (a third-party email forwarding service) before reaching the operator; their anti-spam measures may include a CAPTCHA. Please review FormSubmit's privacy information before submitting. Do not send sensitive personal information through ordinary email.

Optional site analytics

If you choose to allow CozyLoops analytics, the website records an article/page identifier, visit date and a pseudonymous identifier derived for that day in a Supabase analytics table to avoid repeat counting. The site's own analytics table is not designed to store visitor names, email addresses or raw IP addresses. The hosting service and other network providers may separately receive technical request information, including IP addresses, as part of providing the service. You may decline analytics and still read the site; withdrawing permission stops future optional tracking in that browser, but does not automatically erase earlier records.

Advertising and third-party technology

CozyLoops already uses third-party advertising technology, including HB Agency. Advertising partners may independently receive technical request data and use cookies, identifiers or similar storage for advertising delivery, measurement, fraud prevention and, where permitted, personalization. The specific providers and purposes may change and must be confirmed against the live ad stack. The site's optional analytics preference does not automatically block or control advertising technologies. Consent for advertising in jurisdictions where required must be handled separately through an appropriate consent management solution.

Images and externally hosted content

Some images may load from external providers, including Pexels and Unsplash, or from CozyLoops' hosted assets. When your browser retrieves an externally hosted image, its provider may receive your IP address, browser information and the referring page URL. AI-generated illustrations are labeled where known.

Cookies, storage and choices

We use browser storage for optional analytics preferences and may use functional session storage for page-view de-duplication. Advertising partners may use additional cookies and local storage independently. See our Cookie Policy for more detail and browser controls.

Optional analytics preferences

These controls affect CozyLoops' own optional page-view analytics. They do not control advertising partners' cookies or replace an advertising consent management platform.

Retention and requests

We retain correspondence only as needed for handling enquiries and legitimate recordkeeping. We aim to retain analytics only for the time needed to understand site use, subject to our technical configuration. For access, correction or deletion requests, contact the operator at Houssam.el.8ur@gmail.com. We will assess requests under applicable law.

Policy changes

This policy is reviewed when new services or data uses are introduced. The last-updated date above identifies this version.
