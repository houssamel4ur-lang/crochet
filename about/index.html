About CozyLoops

Last updated: November 16, 2026

Welcome to CozyLoops, a small corner of the internet for people who enjoy making something useful or lovely with yarn. We publish free crochet patterns, project ideas, and step-by-step learning resources. You do not need a subscription or purchase to read our patterns.

Who this is for

CozyLoops is for curious beginners, returning makers, and anyone who wants to explore crochet at a comfortable pace. Projects range from small ornaments and amigurumi-inspired ideas to household accents and miniature crochet work. Where possible, a guide explains materials, US stitch abbreviations, stitch counts, shaping, and finishing so you can understand the construction rather than simply copy a picture.

How we prepare our guides

We aim to make instructions clear, check written counts and construction logic where possible, and correct issues readers report. An editorial review is not the same as making a physical sample. Some articles contain original, not-yet-physically-tested pattern drafts; they must not be presented as completed or verified projects. We label concept illustrations as such, and a generated image is never proof that a particular set of stitches produces the pictured item.

Before starting a new project, read the full instructions, make a gauge swatch when fit matters, and use appropriate safety precautions, especially for items intended for children. Measurements can vary with yarn, hook, and tension. We want readers to be able to adapt a draft thoughtfully and give useful feedback.

Free patterns and editorial independence

Our tutorials are free to read. Advertising may appear on the site; an ad does not mean a recommended material or technique has been independently tested. If affiliate partnerships are introduced, applicable links will be identified clearly. You can read more in our Disclosure and Privacy Policy.
