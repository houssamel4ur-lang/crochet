Cookie Policy

Last updated: November 17, 2026

Cookies are small files websites can store in a browser. Local and session storage are other ways websites remember settings. This page describes the known first-party analytics choices and the possibility of cookies from advertising partners.

CozyLoops browser storage

The site uses cozyloops_analytics_consent to remember whether you allowed or declined optional first-party analytics. When enabled, temporary browser identifiers and visit-deduplication markers may be used to count a page view once per day. Our Supabase analytics table is designed to store a pseudonymous daily hash, date and page identifier, not visitor names or email addresses. A web host or third party may separately process technical network data.

Advertising cookies and partners

CozyLoops displays third-party advertising using HB Agency and associated technology. These partners may use their own cookies or similar identifiers for ad delivery, measurement, and other permitted purposes. Because advertising technology can change, do not treat the CozyLoops analytics preference as consent for advertising or a way to disable ad cookies. Where legally required, an appropriate separate ad-consent mechanism must be in place.

Manage optional first-party analytics

Optional CozyLoops analytics should be off until you actively allow them. You can change your preference here at any time. A choice made on one browser does not automatically apply on another device.

Optional analytics preferences

These controls affect CozyLoops' own optional page-view analytics. They do not control advertising partners' cookies or replace an advertising consent management platform.

Advertising consent preferences

CozyLoops' analytics choice does not control advertising partners. HB Agency may provide an InMobi Choice consent manager, whose preferences screen is available only when its provider integration and regional rules permit it. These buttons ask the installed provider to open its own controls; pressing them does not grant, reject, or change advertising consent on its own.

Manage cookies in your browser

Most browsers let you block or delete cookies and site data in their privacy settings. Blocking essential storage can affect saved preferences. For third-party advertising choices, use the advertising consent controls displayed on the site when available, or appropriate browser/privacy controls.
